MLB Attendance and TV Research

What this explores

What drives MLB attendance, why specific teams and games beat or miss expectations, and whether smaller crowds went with smaller national TV audiences.

Who it is for

Sports media and sponsorship sellers preparing team pitches.

How to use it

Ask about a team, season, game or comparison, then ask follow-ups in the same conversation. A vague question gets one clarifying question first. Answers take up to about 2 minutes, and every fact shows its source.

Attendance covers the 2024 to 2026 regular seasons. National TV audiences cover 2025 and 2026, and the 2025 figures end in early September. Local TV viewership and the postseason are not included.
