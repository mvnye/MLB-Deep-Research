MLB Attendance and Viewership Research

    This tool helps sports media and sponsorship sellers build team pitches from MLB crowd and TV data. Ask about a specific team, season, game, or comparison. A team of research agents looks up attendance and national TV audiences, compares each team and game with what its usual crowd would predict, and adds context from Wikipedia when something stands out. The answer comes back with sources and a chart (when helpful). Vague questions will ask for clarification.

    Attendance covers the 2024 to 2026 regular seasons. National TV audiences cover 2025 and 2026, and the 2025 figures end in early September. Local TV viewership and the postseason are not included.
